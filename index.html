Located at the heart of Wan Chai

7 Hing Wan Street a Chinese styled tenement block located within the heritage area and right opposite to a historical building. Within 3 minutes walk from the modern shopping and dining hub along Queen’s Road East, residents can access main office towers, parks, hotels and international conference and exhibition center in town through various means of public transport within stone’s throw.

By exercising best practices in conservation and adaptive reuse, the building is refurbished into 16 spacious and fully fitted apartments with modern provisions and housekeeping services. Flexible term from 1 month is available.
